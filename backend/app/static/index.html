How to shoot it (read this first)
  1. Put it on newspaper or printed text. A patterned sheet lets the software work out where the phone was.
  2. Shiny or plain plastic? Dust it first. Dry shampoo, talc, or foot powder, thin and even. Shine and blank color can't be measured.
  3. Bright, soft light, no flash. Near a window or under a ceiling light works. Avoid hard shadows.
  4. Move the phone, never the piece. Don't touch or bump the sheet between shots.
  5. Take 30 to 50 photos in 3 circles: low, middle, and high, a small step between shots. Each one should overlap the last by about two thirds.
  6. Fill about a third of the frame. Hold still and tap the piece to focus. Keep the same zoom the whole time, and don't use the ultra-wide lens.
  7. Measure the longest side with a ruler or calipers. That one number sets the real size of the whole model.
The bottom touching the sheet can't be seen. With AI gap fill on, it gets filled in and marked as not measured. Otherwise it stays open.
